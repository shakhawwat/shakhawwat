## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **Applied AI Engineer and AI Researcher** focused on building intelligent systems that combine research with real-world engineering.

My work spans **Large Language Models, Natural Language Processing, Multimodal AI, Computer Vision, AI Agents, RAG, automation, IoT, and robotics**.

I enjoy taking ideas from **research → experimentation → implementation → deployment**, with particular interest in intelligent agents, transformer-based architectures, knowledge-enhanced AI, multimodal systems, and autonomous applications.

* 🔭 Currently building **LLM-powered agents, AI automation systems, RAG pipelines, and intelligent SaaS applications**
* 🧠 Researching **LLMs, NLP, Multimodal AI, Transformers, Knowledge-Enhanced Learning, and Deep Learning**
* 🤖 Building AI-powered **robotics, agriculture automation, IoT, and intelligent monitoring systems**
* 🧪 Interested in **SOTA AI research and reproducible experimentation**
* ⚙️ Working with **Python, APIs, FastAPI, LLM frameworks, vector databases, Docker, and n8n**
* 📚 Published research involving **transformer architectures, emotion classification, Arabic text classification, and cyberbullying detection**
* 🤝 Open to **AI research collaboration, Applied AI engineering, NLP, LLM, multimodal AI, automation, robotics, and open-source projects**
* 🌍 Based in **Bangladesh**
